NETFLIX — PHOTOS
================

Drop your real photos here. Name each one to match a memory, and it
will show up automatically:

    summer-trip.jpg        (Summer Trip)
    golden-hour.jpg        (Golden Hour)
    late-night-talks.jpg   (Late Night Talks)
    tiny-moments.jpg       (Tiny Moments)
    that-laugh.jpg         (That Laugh)

If a photo is missing, a beautiful animated poster shows instead,
so nothing ever looks broken. Your photo just swaps in when you add it.

Good size: 800–2000 px wide. .jpg, .png, .webp all work.
