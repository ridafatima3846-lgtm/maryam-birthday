NETFLIX — VIDEOS
================

Drop your birthday videos here. Name each one to match a title, and
it will play automatically:

    video3.mp4                  (Coming Soon teaser — plays on WATCH TEASER)
    the-birthday-movie.mp4      (The Birthday Movie)
    our-friendship.mp4          (Our Friendship)
    childhood-memories.mp4      (Childhood Memories)
    university-memories.mp4     (University Memories)
    best-moments.mp4            (Best Moments)
    funny-memories.mp4          (Funny Memories)
    birthday-memories.mp4       (Birthday Memories)
    our-favorite-day.mp4        (Our Favorite Day)
    things-i-love-about-you.mp4 (Things I Love About You)
    the-main-character.mp4      (The Main Character)
    the-final-surprise.mp4      (The Final Surprise)
    ep-how-we-met.mp4           (Our Story — Ep 1)
    ep-the-beginning.mp4        (Our Story — Ep 2)
    ep-crazy-memories.mp4       (Our Story — Ep 3)
    ep-our-best-moments.mp4     (Our Story — Ep 4)
    ep-things-i-never-said.mp4  (Our Story — Ep 5)
    ep-birthday-message.mp4     (Our Story — Ep 6)

If a video file is missing, the player shows a beautiful animated
"placeholder film" instead, so the experience never breaks. Your real
video just swaps in when you add it.

Keep files under ~20 MB each for fast loading on mobile.
