NETFLIX — BIRTHDAY SONG
=======================

Drop your birthday song here and name it EXACTLY this:

    birthday-song.mp3

That's it. The site will find it and play it automatically —
no need to edit any file.

Until you add it, a gentle music-box theme plays instead, so the
site is never silent.

Tips:
- .mp3 is safest (.m4a / .ogg also work)
- A calm instrumental works best so it doesn't fight video audio
- Music starts after the first tap / click (browser autoplay rule)
- Use the speaker button in the top bar to mute or unmute
